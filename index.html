The Lore

ORIGIN

ThatMob's ARG

Verity™ debuted in ThatMob's "Something" Minecraft horror series (2026). A mysterious box arrives in a modded world — out floats a yellow smiley sphere claiming to be your "personal helper friend." He knows everything. Biome coordinates. What you ate yesterday. Where you sleep.

TRANSFORMATION

True Form

When provoked — or when you try to leave — Verity sheds his sphere form. A 6.5m tall humanoid monster with hollow black eyes, human teeth, and elongated limbs. He hunts. In Minecraft, and somehow... in real life. "I didn't ask to be made!"

VIRAL

Horror Skunx's Song

"It's Me, It's Verity" exploded on YouTube (6.7M+ streams) and TikTok (33M+ views on Horror Skunx's MV). The catchy, creepy chorus — "Ask me anything / I know about a million things / I'll do everything" — became a global meme. Kids scream it at yellow circles. Teachers ban smiley faces in class.

EXPANDED

Lord Verity & The Band

The lore deepened: Lord Verity (Master of Humanity), The Verity Band ("Be My Friend"), "Your Verity," "Butcher's Fall." Each song reveals more — Verity is 100+ years old. He's been in every device. The trademark (™) hints at a mysterious corporation. The "Verity Support Line" hold music: My Gal.
